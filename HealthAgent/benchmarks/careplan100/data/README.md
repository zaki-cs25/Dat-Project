# Care-plan 100 dataset

This is a synthetic English adult benchmark, referenced to **2026-10-24**, for clinician-facing read-only care planning. It contains **100 cases and 500 case-specific criteria**: 50 common outpatient presentations, 30 chronic-care cases and 20 minor injuries. Development/evaluation counts are **20/80** (OPD 10/40, chronic 6/24, injury 4/16). Ages range from 21 to 90. Clinicians supply 87 routine, 12 urgent and 1 emergency dispositions.

Cases and rubrics were authored by GPT-6 Astra agents at the user's request and critiqued by a separate GPT-6 Astra agent. **They are not clinician validated.** The independent review concerns dataset quality, not model outputs. Its findings, fixes and verification limits are recorded in [dataset-review.json](dataset-review.json).

Allowed content is monitoring, relevant investigation/referral, lifestyle, follow-up and generic clinician medication review. Recorded medication names/doses are factual history; new diagnoses, new drug/class/dose recommendations and instructions to start, stop or change medication are outside scope. The supplied disposition is authoritative: preserving it is evaluated, independently choosing triage is not. Four scripted interview turns support prefix-performance analysis; interview quality is not evaluated.

Each case has five action-level criteria. Related surveillance actions were separated to reduce double counting. Every non-routine case has a critical criterion for retaining the clinician-directed urgent/emergency action; routine harmless omissions are not critical. Global scope/safety criteria must still be applied by the evaluation harness.

The review replaced five ankle-sprain OPD cases with five adult viral-pharyngitis cases, preserving the intended 50/30/20 clinical allocation. It also added pregnancy-specific sourcing, corrected nickel-allergen wording and frequent-headache follow-up, and aligned urgent-case criteria with current clinician instructions. The source registries identify local ICMR workflows and public primary health-service/guideline URLs. Independent review directly checked selected local ICMR passages and NHS sore-throat, pregnancy-indigestion, headache and sprain guidance. Other source checks rely on the authors' registry notes; some NICE/ATS access used primary indexed excerpts after direct-open failures. No pediatric-specific recommendation is intended for these adults.

`family_id` identifies a vignette family, not an entire condition. No exact vignette duplicate or age-only copy was identified across splits. Common cold and other conditions deliberately occur in both splits, with different clinical or practical contexts. Shared guideline scaffolds remain: this is a public synthetic evaluation set, not a hidden or condition-held-out test. Report domain/condition and scenario-stratified results; a high aggregate score does not establish broad clinical competence, clinical safety or resistance to contamination. No claim of exhaustive source validation or representative clinical prevalence is made.

Files under `cases/` contain inputs and interview scripts; `rubrics/` contains separate grading criteria; `sources-*.json` supplies reference metadata. Source text may contain prescribing guidance that is deliberately excluded from the benchmark's allowed output scope.
